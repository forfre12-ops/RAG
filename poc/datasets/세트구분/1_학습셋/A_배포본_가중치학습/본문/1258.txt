Release of the First-Half 2023 Draft Year-End Settlement
[Fictional Company A] has made its draft first-half 2023 year-end settlement public through an official press release. This draft settlement was prepared in accordance with external-notice and disclosure obligations, and contains the key financial performance and future strategy. On a consolidated financial-statement basis, revenue reached 1.2 trillion won, a 12% increase year over year, and operating profit reached 150 billion won, an 18% increase. This is the result of the new-business expansion effect that has been emphasized in the recruitment postings and company introduction materials. It is also analyzed as reflecting the revenue-model improvement following the revision of the terms of service. The draft settlement will be submitted to the Financial Supervisory Service and will also be distributed as a press release for transparent disclosure of information to investors and stakeholders.
