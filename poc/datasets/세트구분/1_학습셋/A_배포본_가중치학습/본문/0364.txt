2025 Personnel Transfer Plan and Core Personnel Protection Measures
This document contains the strategy for establishing [Fictional Company A]'s 2025 personnel transfer plan and core personnel protection measures. In particular, in divisions related to national core technology and defense industry technology, transfer-restriction clauses will be applied to holders of the autonomous-driving core algorithm and the RLHF reward model weights. During personnel transfers, the movement of personnel related to next-generation product blueprints and the semiconductor process recipe linked to the M&A plan must undergo a prior security review, and the EUV process parameter and new drug candidate substance development teams are recommended for reorganization within their existing departments. In addition, holders of cryptographic algorithm keys and the HSM master seed, root CA private key administrators, and others must undergo strengthened security authentication procedures upon transfer, and the zero-day vulnerability analysis team is included among the transfer-restricted. Personnel transfers associated with the acquisition due diligence report are strictly controlled until the non-public merger price and the non-public IPO schedule are disclosed. Among personnel currently included in the executive evaluation system, only developers of the Phase 1 clinical trial results and battery cathode material composition technology are eligible for transfer, while for the solid-state electrolyte composition research team, the conclusion of a security agreement is mandatory upon transfer. This security plan, in conjunction with the protection of the foundation model pre-training dataset, ensures that all personnel transfers are thoroughly managed in accordance with internal security protocols.
