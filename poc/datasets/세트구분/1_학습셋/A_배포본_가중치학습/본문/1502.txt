2024 [Company A] Business Plan: A Strategy for Market Expansion Through Innovative Technology
Through its FY2024 business plan, [Company A] is unveiling a business model based on new technology. This document is intended to be used as an external announcement and press release, and includes a systematic overhaul of the company overview and terms of service. As a key strategy, the company plans to introduce an AI-based customer analytics system and to conduct partnership review based on the investment proposal. In particular, it will strengthen its external-announcement strategy for securing new personnel through job postings, and will establish measures to minimize the legal risks of the existing terms of service. This business plan was established through consultation between the internal strategic planning team and outside experts, and aims at external release in the interest of transparent management.
