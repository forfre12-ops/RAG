Company A Announces FY2024 New Business Expansion Plan
Company A has announced the development of an AI-based customer analytics platform—built on [Company A]'s core competencies—as a new business area in Q3 2024. This business expansion was officially disclosed through a press release and is being carried out in parallel with external announcements and a revision of the terms of service. Going forward, the company plans to secure specialized personnel in the relevant field through job postings, and to emphasize its technological superiority via the company introduction page. This aligns with the strategic direction set out in the business plan and reflects the core contents of the investment proposal derived from the market analysis.
