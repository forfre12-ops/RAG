FY2024 [Company A] Business Plan
This document is a press release containing [Company A]'s major business strategy for FY2024, and is used as an external announcement and as disclosure material for investors. Along with a business expansion plan based on an investment proposal for new-market entry, it sets out a strategy that reflects the results of the existing partnership review. In particular, it includes the schedule of job postings for the digital transformation project and a plan to produce a company introduction video, and a review of the proposed revision of the terms of service is also underway. This plan is to be distributed to external partners and the press after approval by management.
