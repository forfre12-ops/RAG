Personnel Transfer Plan (Top Secret)
[Fictional Company A] carefully manages personnel transfers to protect top secret-grade core proprietary technology. Where personnel working in the defense industry technology and national core technology development divisions handle cryptographic algorithm key and HSM master seed management duties, consultation with the security department is mandatory. In particular, personnel transfers in the EUV process parameter research team and the autonomous-driving core algorithm development team are subject to additional security verification procedures upon staff redeployment. This personnel transfer plan also includes the redeployment of the zero-day vulnerability response team, and the relevant personnel must complete training on the protection of the foundation model pre-training dataset.
